Our safety promise

These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every activity, for every guest.

  • We brief every guest before every activity.
  • We inspect helmets, life jackets and harnesses before every use.
  • We match activities to each guest's age, fitness and experience.
  • We keep a close eye on weather and river conditions.
  • We reschedule whenever conditions aren't safe.
