Attractions

Pick your adventure

From heart-pounding drops to gentle family rides, there’s something for every kind of explorer.

🎢 Extreme

Thunder Loop

Our tallest coaster: a 62 m drop, three inversions and speeds up to 110 km/h.

🌊 All ages

Splash Canyon

Twelve water slides, a wave pool and a lazy river winding through the canyon.

🌲 Thrilling

Canopy Zip Line

Fly 1.2 km across the treetops on the longest zip line in the region.

🎡 All ages

Sky Wheel

Glide 55 m above the park for sweeping views — magical at sunset.

🧗 Thrilling

Summit Wall

Twenty climbing routes from beginner to expert, with friendly guides on hand.

🎆 All ages

Night Lights

Every evening the park glows with a parade, light show and fireworks finale.
