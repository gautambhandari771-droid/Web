Safety first

Every adventure starts with a safety check

Adventure should feel wild, never reckless. Before anyone gets on the water, into a harness or onto a trail, our team works through the same checks, every time.

  • Experienced guides

    A team backed by 25 years of adventure experience.

  • Quality-checked gear

    Helmets, life jackets and harnesses are inspected before every use.

  • Briefing for everyone

    Every guest learns what to expect and what to do before starting.

  • Conditions checked

    We watch the weather and river levels, and reschedule if it isn't safe.

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Get in touch and we'll help.

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Many of our activities are beginner friendly, and our team will recommend the right ones for you when you book.

How do you keep guests safe?

Every activity starts with a safety briefing, uses equipment that's inspected before each use and is led by experienced guides. If the weather or river conditions aren't safe, we reschedule.

Are there age or health requirements?

Some activities have age, weight or health requirements. Share your details when you book and we'll tell you what's suitable for everyone in your group.

Do prices change in peak season?

Yes. From March to June, rafting costs 15–20% more on weekends and crowded days, and camping and hotel prices rise with demand. We confirm the exact price when you book.

Are there lower prices for children or students?

Children aged 6–11 pay half the adult price for camping, and students pay ₹1,800 instead of ₹2,000 for the zip line.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, sturdy shoes, sunscreen and a change of clothes for water activities. We'll share a full list for your activities when you book.

When is the best time to visit?

It depends on the activity, as river activities are seasonal. Ask us when you book and we'll suggest the best dates for your trip.
